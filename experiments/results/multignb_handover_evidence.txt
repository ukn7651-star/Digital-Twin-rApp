## Method note for paper
Handovers are triggered via OAI CU telnet (ci trigger_f1_ho), not autonomous A3/CIO
or FlexRIC E2-RC. The rApp's steering decision (which UEs to move) comes from the
analytical twin; the real stack executes the chosen handovers with this scripted trigger.
